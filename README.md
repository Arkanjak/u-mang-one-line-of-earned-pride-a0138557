A make.fun entry by Arkanjak for the [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557) bounty: one line, 1 to 240 characters, giving the judge a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene.

`main.ts` (Node 22, deterministic, no network) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The old fisherman mended the same torn net for the third morning running, and when it finally held, he just nodded at the sea and went home.

Run it with `node main.ts`. Referral code: `ne0pfbnn`.
